RECOMMENDATIONS
1. Keep the top revenue products always in stock: Wheat Flour 5kg, Rice 5kg, Moong Dal 1kg, Sunflower Oil 1L, Toor Dal 1kg.
2. Grains is the strongest category (30.7% of sales); protect its supply and pricing.
3. Increase stock before Sunday and weekends; keep lower stock on Thursday.
4. Offer bundle deals or discounts on slow products: Turmeric Powder 200g, Salt 1kg, Bath Soap, Biscuits Pack, Instant Noodles.
5. Plan extra stock for the best month (2025-03) and reduce orders in the weakest month (2026-06).
6. Walk-in customers give 72.4% of sales; start loyalty offers to make them regular.
7. UPI is the most used payment method (49.5%); promote digital payments.
8. Reorder class A products weekly, class B every two weeks and class C monthly.
